Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit manuscriptos.com. It applies to this website; separate notices may apply to the manuscriptOS beta application.

Information we collect

This website does not require an account and does not directly use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and request details to operate and secure the service.

If you choose to join the beta, you will be directed to Apple's TestFlight service. Apple may process information associated with your participation according to its own privacy practices.

How information is used

Information made available through beta participation may be used to provide beta access, respond to requests, improve manuscriptOS, prevent abuse, and communicate relevant product updates.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website and beta program, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We retain submitted information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your choices

You can choose not to join the beta. You may also ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you previously provided, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as manuscriptOS evolves. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact information made available through the manuscriptOS beta.
